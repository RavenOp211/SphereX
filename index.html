About the pictures. The sky is simulated from each target's catalog position and measured motion, with eight example dates between mid-2025 and late 2026. Junior Space Explorer Manual

How to explore SPHEREx in 3 easy steps

Think of this website like a giant spaceship cockpit! You can fly around our planets in 3D, or peek through a magic rainbow telescope that sees invisible heat in space.

1. Fly Around Use Space tour to visit planets in 3D
2. Switch Lenses Use Sky lab to spot hidden heat
3. Click Dots Tap any star to learn its secret story
4. Pin Notes Save cool findings on the sticky board
1Flying in Space tour

This is the 3D Solar System screen you see when the site first opens.

  • Look Around: Click and drag anywhere on the screen to spin around the Sun.
  • Zoom Closer: Scroll your mouse wheel (or pinch with two fingers) to zoom right up to a planet.
  • Blast Off: Tap any floating name—or a button at the bottom—to fly straight to that planet!
2Using the Sky lab

Click Sky lab at the top to look through SPHEREx's special space telescope.

  • Pick a Spot: Choose a target button at the top (like Barnard's Star) to point the telescope.
  • Press Play: Hit the round Play button under the sky picture to watch stars move across time!
  • Tap Any Dot: Click any glowing dot in the picture to see what it is and how fast it travels.
3What do the 6 Lenses do?

Human eyes can only see normal light, but SPHEREx sees infrared heat! Slide through Lenses 1 to 6 to make different space objects glow.

Lens 1 Lens 2 Lens 3 Lens 4 Lens 5 Lens 6
Lenses 1 & 2 · Bright Stars

Great for spotting normal hot stars. The sky looks full and sparkly here!

Lenses 3 & 4 · Galaxies & Ice

Great for finding faraway swirling galaxies and giant clouds of frozen water in space.

Lenses 5 & 6 · Cold Hidden Worlds

Bright stars fade away here, so chilly brown dwarfs and space rocks finally pop out!

4What do the buttons in Sky lab do?

Colour mix

Paints invisible heat in bright rainbow colours—hot stars look blue and cool objects glow red.

Spot change

Hides all the still stars so only blinking stars and moving space rocks stay visible.

Trails

Connects all 8 pictures together into dotted lines so you can see fast stars zooming across space.

Brightness & Zoom

Turn up Brightness boost to see super-faint galaxies, or slide Zoom out to see a wider patch of sky.

5Try a 1-click space mission!

Want us to set up the telescope for you? Tap any mission button below and watch it work!
